# Global Travel & Expense (T&E) Guidelines

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA**
>
> This document contains entirely fictional policies created exclusively for the KOHLER-MITWPU AI Research Lab AI Challenge prototype. It does not represent actual operational guidelines, policies, or financial frameworks of KOHLER or any other real-world organization.

**Document ID:** POL-FIN-2026-042  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Access Classification:** Internal — Employee Only  
**Applies To:** All full-time permanent and contracted corporate staff globally.

## 1. Business Travel Eligibility

- All business travel must be pre-approved by a direct line manager at least 10 business days before departure through the internal corporate portal.
- Travel is authorized for client-facing meetings, mandatory annual company summits, cross-office deployment projects, or accredited professional certification workshops.
- Personal days appended to business trips must be declared in advance. Personal expenses during extended stays are not reimbursable.

## 2. Accommodation & Hotel Limits

Nightly hotel rates exclude local taxes and resort fees.

- **Domestic Tier-1 Metros:** Maximum USD $275 per night.
- **Domestic Standard Cities:** Maximum USD $175 per night.
- **International High-Cost Regions:** Maximum USD $320 per night.
- **International Standard Regions:** Maximum USD $210 per night.

Booking a property above these caps without prior Vice President (VP) written approval results in claim rejection.

## 3. Daily Meal Allowances

Daily meal allowances are provided on a per-diem basis.

- **Breakfast:** Maximum USD $20.
- **Lunch:** Maximum USD $35.
- **Financial/Client Dinner:** Maximum USD $75.
- **Total Daily Meal Cap:** USD $130.
- Client dinners require a named attendee list and business justification.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 4. Local Transportation

- Standard ride-sharing services and metered taxis are authorized for travel between airports, hotels, and offices.
- Luxury transportation requires authorization when applicable.
- Rental cars must be Economy or Compact class unless director-level approval is obtained.
- Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at **USD $0.67 per mile**, up to **300 miles per round trip**.

## 5. Flight Class Rules

- **Domestic flights under 6 hours:** Economy class only.
- **International flights of 6 hours or more:** Premium Economy is authorized.
- **Business Class:** Restricted to C-Suite executives and approved medical accommodation cases.
- Unauthorized Business Class bookings may result in personal liability for the price difference.

## 6. Receipt Requirements

- Itemized receipts or verified digital invoices are required for individual expenses exceeding **USD $25**.
- Credit-card slips showing only the total amount are not sufficient.
- Receipts must be uploaded to the expense portal within **14 calendar days** of trip completion.

## 7. Approval Workflow

- **Under USD $500:** Automated manager verification.
- **USD $500–$5,000:** Direct manager digital signature.
- **Above USD $5,000:** Department Head and Regional Finance Controller approval.

## 8. Reimbursement Timeline

Approved expense reports processed by the finance team before 5:00 PM local time on Fridays are disbursed through the standard bi-weekly payroll cycle, typically within **5–7 business days**.

## 9. Policy Exceptions

Emergency exceptions, including medical emergencies, sudden geopolitical crises, or airline cancellations that strand an employee, must be reported to the Global Travel Desk within **24 hours**.

Fraudulent receipts or deliberate violations may trigger a compliance review, disciplinary action, and revocation of corporate card privileges.
