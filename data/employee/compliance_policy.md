## 1. Confidential Information

Employees must protect confidential company information from unauthorized
access, disclosure, or distribution.

Confidential information may include internal financial information,
business plans, technical information, customer information, and
non-public company documents.

## 2. Handling Customer Information

Customer information must only be accessed and used for legitimate
business purposes.

Employees must not share customer information with unauthorized people or
external parties.

## 3. Suspicious Requests

Employees who receive an unusual request for confidential information
should not disclose the information until the request has been verified.

When appropriate, employees should escalate the request to their manager,
security team, legal team, or designated compliance contact.

## 4. Data Sharing

Internal or confidential documents should only be shared with users who
have the appropriate authorization.

Employees should verify the recipient before sending confidential
information.

## 5. Security Incident Reporting

Suspected unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, or loss of
confidential company information should be reported through the
designated security or compliance reporting process.

## 6. Policy Questions

Employees who are uncertain whether information can be shared should seek
guidance from the appropriate compliance, legal, security, or management
contact before disclosing it.
