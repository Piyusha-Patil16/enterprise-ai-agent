## 1. Annual Leave

Eligible employees receive **24 days of annual paid leave per calendar year**.

Leave should normally be requested through the company's designated HR
system before the planned absence.

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees may take sick leave when they are unable to work due to illness.

For an absence of more than **3 consecutive working days**, supporting
medical documentation may be requested according to applicable company
procedures.

## 3. Work From Home

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to **2 days per week**,
subject to manager approval and role requirements.

Remote work availability does not apply automatically to every role.

## 4. Parental Leave

Eligible employees may request parental leave in accordance with company
policy and applicable local requirements.

Employees should contact HR for eligibility and documentation requirements.

## 5. Leave Approval

Planned leave should be submitted to the employee's manager through the
designated HR workflow.

Managers are responsible for reviewing requests based on business
requirements and employee eligibility.

## 6. HR Support

Employees who need clarification about leave eligibility, documentation,
or special circumstances should contact the HR team.

## 7. Confidentiality

Employee HR information is internal and must not be disclosed to
unauthorized users.

Customer-facing users do not have access to this document.
