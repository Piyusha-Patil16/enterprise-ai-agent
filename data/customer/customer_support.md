## 1. Product Support

Customers can seek assistance for product installation, basic troubleshooting, warranty questions, and general product information.

## 2. Installation Support

Customers should consult the product installation documentation provided with their product. If installation assistance is required, customer support can provide guidance on available support channels.

## 3. Warranty Support

Warranty-related questions require the product model and purchase information so that eligibility can be reviewed.

## 4. Troubleshooting

For basic troubleshooting, customers should first consult the product documentation. If the issue remains unresolved, customer support can assist with the next steps.

## 5. Support Escalation

Issues that cannot be resolved through standard troubleshooting may be escalated to a specialist support team.
